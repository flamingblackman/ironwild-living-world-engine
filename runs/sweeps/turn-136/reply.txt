IRONWILD FORK A · TURN 136 · TICK 12

The Beacon Year — Forging the Vanguard

The Great Basin's rebuilt defenses have held against another series of attacks. Inside the Legion Engineering Grounds, the air smells of hot metal, welding smoke, and freshly cut armor plate.

A heavy metallic CLANG rings through the yard.

Vance stands beside Skytalon's newly patched chassis, inspecting the seams where the adult Shadow-Owl's radar-absorbent armor meets its restored plating.

"She's ready for field work again," Vance says, wiping black grease from his hands. "But Sledge and Strider still need attention."

Nearby, Gold Sledge's massive black-and-gold frame rests beneath the engineering gantry. Its Titanium-Cestus catches the furnace light. The gorilla Bulwark remains exhausted, but its neural Sentient Seat is responsive.

Queen Kaelen approaches the central drafting table.

"The city is fortified. The patrols are holding. We've bought ourselves room to build something better."

She spreads out the drafted doctrine for the elite rapid-response force.

"We know what this force is supposed to protect. Settlements. Caravans. Wounded Engines. But we haven't selected its personnel or built its equipment."

Vance looks toward the repair bays.

"And every engineer we put on new equipment is one we can't put on recovering the existing Pack."

Kaelen nods.

"Then the question belongs to our King."

Beyond the engineering yard, the ordinary work of rebuilding continues. No fresh alarm sounds. No enemy has breached the walls.

The decision is what deserves Brigus's attention now.

What does Brigus do?
