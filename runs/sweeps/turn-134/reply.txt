IRONWILD · FORK A · TURN 134

The Beacon's Shield

Great Basin — Legion Engineering Grounds

The foundries glow against the morning darkness. Hammer blows echo through the engineering district, while patrol horns sound from the newly fortified walls.

Inside the workshop, Vance and Tull stand over the engineers' competing designs.

The King's decision settles the debate.

An elite rapid-response force. Not an assault force.

The mission is to protect settlements, caravans, and wounded Engines.

Vance takes the assault formation drawings and pushes them aside.

"Then we're building something different. Fighters who get there before the fighting becomes a massacre."

Tull nods.

"People on the road shouldn't have to wait for the entire Legion to mobilize. Neither should a wounded Engine."

Queen Kaelen examines the plans.

"And the city cannot be left undermanned to accomplish this. We have six hundred soldiers, not six thousand."

The proposed unit doctrine

The engineers begin drafting a three-part operational structure.

1. Road Response

Reach threatened caravans, escort traveling families, and secure dangerous stretches of road until help arrives.

2. Settlement Defense

Reinforce vulnerable settlements, respond to raids, and protect civilians during evacuations.

3. Engine Recovery Protection

Establish defensive perimeters around wounded Feral Engines, protect their recovery, and hold attackers back until their Pack can respond.

The first engineering decision

Vance proposes selecting experienced volunteers from the existing Legion rather than expanding beyond its six hundred members.

Tull wants candidates who have demonstrated discipline under pressure, understand how Feral Engines behave, and can operate without depending on the Pack to rescue them.

Kaelen adds one condition:

"Ordinary patrols must remain staffed. We aren't sacrificing the kingdom's security to create another military formation."

The engineers begin preparing selection criteria and an equipment requirements document.

There is no grand new army marching out of the foundry today.

There is something more important: a clear purpose for the force.

A shield that moves. A force that arrives. A kingdom that protects its own.

The engineers await the next stage of authorization before selecting personnel or spending salvage.

MECHANICAL BOUNDARY
