# WorldRenewal — Plain Language Version
Date: 2026-11-22. This is the plain-language version of docs/worldrenewal-plan.md. No code, no world changes, plan only.

## The problem, plain
The world looks busy, but it runs out of story. After the first few days, the only thing that keeps happening is the same loop: the Breaker chief nets Ashfang, Pike fixes him, repeat. No clocks move. No raids land. No trade happens. Five out of twenty things the world can do never happen at all. After that, the world just scavenges, claims, and rests, forever.

## The goal, plain
Make the world keep making new story, for 2,000 days, not just the first few. A 2,000-day test run should have real, meaningful events in every 100-day window, not counting the net-and-fix loop. Clean, repeatable, all tests passing.

What counts as a meaningful event? A clock moving or finishing, a place changing hands, a raid actually gaining something, a new place being claimed, a status changing, a bond changing. That definition still needs Bryan's yes.

## The 8 builds, plain

A. Clocks that can repeat.
Right now a clock finishes once and dies forever. Change it so some clocks finish, do their thing, and start over again. Example: the Dust-Baron clock and the caravan route clock could keep cycling, instead of stopping dead.

B. Things wear down over time.
Right now resources sit still forever. Add a slow drift, every 30 days or so, something small happens: a faction loses some food to spoilage, a place gets a bit run down. Nothing touches Ragnus, Ashfang, or Pike. Just the world around them, slowly shifting.

C. Wake up the dead clocks.
Right now Fork B has three clocks, and none of them have a working way to move. Give each one a real reason to move. Pike fixing Ashfang at home moves the bond clock. A successful trade moves the caravan route clock. A raid attempt moves the Dust-Baron clock. This build also needs the repair fix Bryan already approved: point Pike's fix at the bond counter.

D. A meter that notices when the world goes quiet.
A measuring tool, not a change to the world. Every 100 days it counts meaningful events and says if the world is still making story, and what kind. If any 100-day window has zero meaningful events, the world is stuck. This is how we test the other builds.

E. Goals that change with pressure.
Right now a faction acts the same whether it is comfortable or starving. Change it so when a faction is low on food, feeding and trading matter more to them, and they actually do those things more. When the pressure is gone, they go back to normal.

F. People age out and hand over.
Right now the same people act forever. Add aging and retirement: an older person retires at a set age, and if they have a named successor, that successor wakes up and takes over the job. If there is no successor, they just stop acting, no crash. Ragnus and Pike never age or retire, that is by design.

G. Eras turn over.
Right now an era ending just closes the books. Change it so when a new era starts, the world turns over: new era name, some clocks reset, each faction's food resets toward a starting amount, not wiped out, just pulled back. Ragnus, Ashfang, and Pike are never reset.

H. New ground to discover.
Right now there are only two places left to discover, then the map runs out. Add a pool of new places, approved by Lore, that appear one at a time when the map runs out, so scouting and claiming never run dry.
